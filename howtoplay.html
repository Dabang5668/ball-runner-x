How To Play

Everything you need to know before you start dodging. Obstacle names and colors change with your chosen World Preset — this is the classic look.
Controls

🕹️ Jump

SPACE ↑ or tap/click — jump. Tap again in mid-air for a double jump.

⬇ Duck

↓ or hold the on-screen duck button to slide under low obstacles.

⏸ Pause

P or ESC to pause and resume anytime.

🔊 Sound FX

M or the on-screen 🔊 button toggles all sound effects on or off instantly.

🎵 Music

N or the on-screen 🎵 button toggles the adaptive background music that speeds up as you level up.

Obstacles (9 types)

Spikes

Low ground obstacle — jump over it.

Wall

Solid brick wall — must jump. Ducking will not save you.

Cave Ceiling

Hangs from the top — the opposite of a wall. Duck to slide safely underneath; jumping into it will hit.

Drone (the "egg")

Floats mid-air — duck underneath it. Its shape and behavior change completely with each World Preset.

Laser

Pulses low along the ground — jump over it.

Fire

Appears in later levels — jump over it.

Buzzsaw

A fast spinning blade on the ground — jump over it, watch for sparks.

Warning Orb

Floats up and down through a wide range. Read its height as it approaches — sometimes you jump, sometimes you duck, sometimes you do nothing.

Swarm

A small cluster of creatures at head height — duck to pass under all of them at once.

Power-Ups

🛡 Shield

Blocks one hit completely — grab it before a tricky obstacle.

⚡ Boost

Temporary speed increase for bigger score gains.

🧲 Magnet

Automatically pulls nearby coins toward you for a limited time.

🕒 Slow-Mo

Temporarily slows the whole run down, giving you more time to react to tricky obstacle patterns.

Scoring & Style

🪙 Coins

Collect coins mid-air for bonus points. Rare golden Mega Coins are worth 5x more.

🔥 Combos

Dodge or collect back-to-back without getting hit to build a combo multiplier.

⚡ Close Calls

Barely avoid an obstacle for a bonus "CLOSE CALL" reward — the closer, the better.

🏆 Milestones

Every 500 points triggers a celebration and screen effect — keep pushing further.

🌍 World Presets

Visit Customize to instantly reskin your ball, background, and every obstacle at once — or mix and match manually.
